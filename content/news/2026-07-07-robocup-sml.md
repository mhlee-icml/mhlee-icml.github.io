TEAM INU from Incheon National University won first place in the Smart Manufacturing League (SML) on its RoboCup debut. The competition took place at Songdo Convensia in Incheon from July 2 to 6, 2026.

Prof. Myoung Hoon Lee participated as one of the team's faculty advisors. The team brought together 16 students from three engineering departments.
