UNITA, the autonomous driving team from Incheon National University's Department of Electrical Engineering, won the Grand Prize at the 1st 2025 KAIST Mobility Challenge, organized by the KAIST Mobility Research Institute. The competition ran from October 15, 2025, to February 10, 2026. The winners were announced on February 10, and UNITA received the top prize of KRW 5 million.

Advised by Prof. Myoung Hoon Lee, the team developed wireless communication-based cooperative driving algorithms for multiple vehicles, together with reliable path planning and tracking. The six members were 이기현, 정우진, 한다인, 송준상, 조재민, and 이다빈.
