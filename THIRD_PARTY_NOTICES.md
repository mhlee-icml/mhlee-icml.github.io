# Theme provenance

This ICML site adapts the Jekyll layout hierarchy, masthead navigation, and
sidebar/content pattern of [Academic Pages](https://github.com/hongsheng-Z/academicpages.github.io).
The inspected upstream files were `_layouts/default.html`, `_layouts/single.html`,
`_includes/masthead.html`, and `LICENSE` on the `master` branch, accessed 2026-10-26.
The lab-specific layouts, CSS, JavaScript, and content preparation scripts were
created for this repository. Upstream example people, publications, and other
demo content are not included.

Academic Pages descends from Michael Rose's Minimal Mistakes theme. Its MIT
notice is retained in [licenses/academic-pages-MIT.txt](licenses/academic-pages-MIT.txt).
The existing root `LICENSE` remains unchanged as the provenance record of the
previous al-folio site.

The original ICML content and image files retain their existing ownership.
